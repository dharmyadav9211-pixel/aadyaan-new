CNC Machining Job Work Services

Our CNC machining job work services take your drawing from raw material to finished component. We work with mild steel, alloy steel, stainless steel, aluminium and brass, for both prototype and production quantities. As a CNC job work provider near me for customers in Taloja, Panvel and Navi Mumbai, we make it easy to get parts machined without long transport times.

CNC Turning and Milling Job Work

CNC turning and milling job work covers shafts, bushes, pins, spacers, flanges, housings, blocks and brackets. Turning handles round parts on the lathe; milling handles flat faces, slots, pockets and holes. Combining both lets us deliver complete parts from one vendor.

CNC Lathe Machine Job Work

Our CNC lathe machine job work is ideal for repeat batches of turned components — shafts, rollers, couplings, threaded parts and bushes — with consistent dimensions across every piece.

5-Axis CNC Machining Job Work

Complex parts with angled holes, contoured surfaces or multiple faces are machined in fewer set-ups with 5-axis CNC machining job work. Fewer set-ups means better accuracy and faster delivery for moulds, impellers, fixtures and aerospace-style components.

CNC Job Works for Aluminium Components

Aluminium needs sharp tooling and the right speeds to get a clean finish. Our CNC job works for aluminum components include enclosures, heat sinks, fixture plates, brackets and automotive parts, supplied ready for anodising or powder coating.

Automotive CNC Machine Shop

As an automotive CNC machine shop, we supply brackets, spacers, mounting plates, jigs, fixtures and replacement parts for automotive component makers, trailer and body builders and workshop tooling.

Custom CNC Manufacturing Shop

Combine machining with our CNC plasma cutting, profile cutting and fabrication and you have a complete custom CNC manufacturing shop under one roof — cut, machine, weld and finish.
