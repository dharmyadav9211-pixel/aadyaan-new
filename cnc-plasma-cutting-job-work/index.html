What is CNC Plasma Cutting?

CNC plasma cutting uses a high-velocity jet of ionised gas (plasma) to melt and blow away metal along a path controlled by a computer. Because the torch follows your CAD/DXF file, every part is cut to the same shape and size — whether you need one piece or one thousand.

At Reliable Steel Weld we offer CNC plasma cutting services and plasma cutting job work on mild steel (MS), stainless steel (SS) and other conductive metals. It is the most economical way to cut medium and thick plates into flanges, base plates, gussets, brackets, rings and complex profiles.

Our CNC Plasma Profile Cutting Job Work

  • CNC plasma profile cutting of any 2D shape from DXF / DWG / PDF drawings
  • MS plate plasma cutting job work for structural and machinery parts
  • Heavy plate cutting job work for base plates, flanges and lifting lugs
  • Stainless steel (SS) plate cutting for food, pharma and chemical plant equipment
  • Nesting optimisation to reduce scrap and lower your cost per part
  • Bevel-ready edges and hole cutting for welding and bolting
  • Customer-supplied material or material supplied by us

Frequently Asked Questions

What files do you need for CNC plasma cutting job work?

A DXF or DWG file is best. We can also work from a PDF, a hand sketch with dimensions, or a sample part — our team will prepare the CNC program for you.

Do you cut customer-supplied plates?

Yes. You can send your own MS or SS plates for job work cutting, or we can supply the material and quote a per-kg or per-piece rate.

How is plasma cutting job work charged?

Plasma cutting job work is usually charged on cutting length and pierce count, or per kg for bulk plate work. Send your drawing on WhatsApp for a quick quotation.

Do you provide plasma cutting services near me in Panvel and Mumbai?

Yes. We are in Taloja MIDC and regularly deliver to Panvel, Kharghar, Kalamboli, Navi Mumbai, Thane and Mumbai.
