CNC Profile Cutting Services

Profile cutting means cutting a flat plate into a specific outline — a flange, a gear blank, a bracket, a base plate or a decorative panel. With our CNC profile cutting services, the machine follows your drawing exactly, giving you clean, repeatable parts without templates or manual marking.

We use CNC plasma and gas (oxy-fuel) profile cutting depending on plate thickness and the finish you need, so you always get the most economical process.

CNC Profile Cutting Job Work — What We Cut

  • Flanges & blind flanges
  • Base plates & anchor plates
  • Gussets, stiffeners & ribs
  • Brackets & clamps
  • Rings, discs & gear blanks
  • Lifting lugs & shackle plates
  • Machine frames & covers
  • Signage & decorative panels

Our CNC profile cutting job work is ideal for both one-off custom parts and repeat production orders.

Custom Metal Profile Cutting

Need something unusual? Custom metal profile cutting is our daily work. Send a sketch, a sample or a DXF file and we will convert it into a CNC program, nest it to save material and cut it to size. Our metal profile cutting services cover mild steel, stainless steel and other conductive metals.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between plasma cutting and profile cutting?

Profile cutting describes the result — cutting a plate to a shape. Plasma cutting is one of the processes used to do it. We choose plasma or gas profile cutting based on your plate thickness and finish.

Is there a minimum order for CNC profile cutting job work?

No. We cut single custom parts as well as bulk production batches.

Can you machine or drill the profiles after cutting?

Yes. We offer drilling, machining, bending and welding so you receive finished parts.
